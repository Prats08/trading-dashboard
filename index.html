What the fund does, and how often

Every trading day
The research team

A team of analysts studies each finalist — price & momentum, valuation, insider flow, sentiment, news, macro and risk. A devil's advocate strikes unsupported claims, a bull and bear argue it out, and a Portfolio Manager decides BUY / SELL / HOLD with a conviction score.

Every Saturday
Weekly directive

The team steps back from the daily tape, grades how last week's calls actually played out, and sets the week's regime and bias — what to lean into, what to avoid. It steers every daily run for the week.

Through the week
The options desk

Strategists scan the universe for risk-defined option structures — long calls and puts, spreads, covered calls and condors — across this-Friday, 30-day and 90-day horizons. A manager ranks and publishes only the best ideas.

Intraday & weekly
Chart signals

Deterministic technical passes — no LLM — scan hourly and daily bars for clean setups, adding a fast, rules-based read alongside the team's judgement.

Daily execution
Autonomous trading

A Chief Investment Officer reviews the team's calls and the week's directive, then proposes one daily trade plan per fund — sized by conviction and each fund's mandate. Inaction is a valid day.

Every order
A deterministic safety gate

Before anything reaches the broker, a pure-code gate enforces holding periods, position and sector caps, a conviction floor, a cash reserve and a daily trade cap. Any order that breaks a limit is rejected and logged — never submitted.
